Shape Shift piano music

Current original arrangements: "Sunny Shapes" (menu, 92 BPM) and "Little Sparks" (gameplay, 104 BPM), created for Shape Shift / MK Playcraft Studio. These replace the previous quiet piano arrangements.

The soft kick, clap and shaker samples were synthesized specifically for these original pop arrangements. No third-party drum recordings or commercial songs are used. Piano Flow uses the same three percussion samples, synchronized to its interactive chart, with major-key piano accompaniment.

Piano recordings: Salamander Grand Piano v3 by Alexander Holm.

Changes: selected recorded notes were trimmed, pitch-shifted by up to two semitones, sequenced into original arrangements, mixed with release envelopes and room reverb, and encoded as stereo Ogg Vorbis loops. These are sampled acoustic-piano arrangements, not recordings of a live pianist playing the complete pieces. No endorsement by the original sample author is implied.

Piano Flow also uses trimmed, Ogg-encoded individual Salamander piano recordings for interactive melody and accompaniment notes. The same attribution and CC BY 3.0 license apply.

4.94.31 original groove arrangements:
Front Porch (menu, 96 BPM), Bright Momentum (main game, 110 BPM), Ribbon Rush (Shape Slice, 118 BPM), Pocket Gravity (Shape Drop, 100 BPM).
Original repeated piano motifs with octave answers, broken chords, synthesized harmonic bass, and the existing original kick/clap/shaker samples. Piano notes use the Salamander recordings credited above. Mixed as 32-bar stereo loops with wrapped release tails and encoded as Ogg Vorbis. No commercial melodies or recordings were imported.

Victory arrangement 4.94.34: original C-major piano fanfare using the credited Salamander samples, pitch-shifted, enveloped and mixed with synchronized firework impacts.

4.94.52 original world menu arrangements:
Waterlight Walk (Sunny Garden, 88 BPM, C major, 32 bars), Lanterns on Still Water (Moonlit Forest, 80 BPM, A minor, 32 bars), Golden Hour Drift (Sunset Islands, 84 BPM, D major, 32 bars).
Original chord-following piano melodies with A/A-variation/B/A phrasing, rolled seventh/ninth voicings, warm synthesized bass and pads, restrained original synthesized percussion, and circular room reflections. Piano notes use the Salamander recordings credited above, pitch shifted, filtered, enveloped, sequenced, and mixed into stereo Ogg Vorbis loops. Release tails wrap across each loop boundary. No commercial melody or recording was imported.
