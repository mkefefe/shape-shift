Shape Shift privacy policy

Updated October 27, 2026. Shape Shift is published by MK Playcraft Studio.

Age and privacy settings

Before online features or the advertising SDK start, Shape Shift asks for an age or offers a choice not to provide it. The age is processed on the device and is not saved or transmitted. Only a privacy category is stored locally. Players under 18 and players who do not provide an age receive the protected experience.

On-device information

Scores, coins, purchases made with game coins, quests, equipment, audio preferences and privacy category are saved on the device. Players can choose a custom name, and adults can choose a photo through the system picker; these custom details remain on the device in this release. Photos are cropped into a small JPEG, and no photo-library permission is requested. Protected profiles use preset avatars. Your chosen nickname is shared on public rankings; choose a name without personal information. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes local progress.

Online rankings

Players of all ages can participate in online rankings, independently of optional gameplay statistics. A random device-specific credential identifies the profile; the service stores its hash, game-run identifiers and seeds, rule version, scores, pick counts and timestamps. Gameplay actions are transmitted to validate scores. Public rankings show your chosen nickname, a preset avatar, an equipped frame and the best score. Chosen nicknames are public; photos are not published by this version. No email, real name or account password is required to play. Network providers necessarily receive connection information such as IP addresses when responding to requests.

Optional gameplay analytics

All editions offer a separate choice to share gameplay statistics. If enabled, a random installation identifier, session identifiers, session timestamps, active/idle/paused time, runs and time by game mode, completion counts, last activity, distribution, game version and coarse device/OS/browser categories are sent to our first-party analytics service. You may optionally share an age group; it is self-reported, not verified. We do not collect your birthday, hardware identifiers, advertising ID, names, photos, or leaderboard identity in this service. The server hashes installation/session identifiers before storage. Installation counts are not exact counts of people and are not combined across websites or apps.

These data are used only for internal game operations and improvements, not advertising profiles or cross-app tracking. Individual analytics records expire after 90 days. Upload retries are kept on your device for up to 24 hours. In Settings → Gameplay statistics you can turn sharing off and request deletion of this installation’s analytics. Developers can mark test devices; those are excluded from normal engagement reports. Hosting providers process connection information to serve requests. Children and unknown-age players are not assumed to be adults. Choosing not to share does not prevent play. Older aggregate-only statistics remain separate.

Advertising

Optional rewarded videos are provided by Google AdMob. Rewards are issued only when the SDK confirms completion. We configure child-directed and under-age-of-consent treatment for protected players and restrict ad content to Google's general-audience rating for everyone. Android advertising-ID access is removed from this build. The SDK may still process other identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. We do not request precise location. Adults receive applicable consent choices through Google's User Messaging Platform. Use Settings > Ad privacy choices to review available choices.

Google's data handling is described in its privacy policy and Mobile Ads SDK disclosure. We do not integrate additional advertising networks or sell game coins for money.

Children

The protected experience permits score submissions using the ranking credential and validation data described above, a chosen public nickname and a preset avatar. It uploads the chosen nickname but does not upload photos. It uses child-protected ad requests. Parents and guardians can use the in-game deletion control for information associated with an installation. Public user-uploaded photos from earlier development versions are no longer served. Please do not put personal information into a game nickname.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you clear it or uninstall. The leaderboard is hosted on Cloudflare. Best scores and profiles remain until deleted; current run-verification records expire after 14 days. Existing verified scores were preserved during migration. In Settings, choose Delete online profile to delete the server profile, its recorded runs and any legacy uploaded avatar. This also disables future ranking uploads on that installation. To join again, choose Settings > Turn on leaderboard uploads and finish a new online Endless run. Deleted scores are not restored. If deletion fails, the app reports the failure and you may retry. See the support page for current help and deletion options. Provider security logs and backups may have separate retention periods; we do not promise their immediate erasure.

Security and service providers

Leaderboard requests use HTTPS. The leaderboard is hosted on Cloudflare. Access to a private profile operation requires the installation's credential. The service and advertising providers may process data in countries other than your own. No system can guarantee complete security.

Your choices and contact

You can use the protected experience, keep photos local, disable online rankings, request deletion, and manage available ad consent choices. For privacy and data-deletion help, contact mk.studio.customersupport@gmail.com. You can also use Settings > Delete online profile to remove your online records. Include your public player ID from Your profile if available; never send your private game credential or a child's photograph. Material changes will be reflected in this policy and, when needed, in the app.

Notifications

Optional reminders are scheduled on this device, at most one a day. Android asks for permission on supported versions. You can deny permission or turn reminders off in Settings. Reminder delivery does not send your profile or progress to a server.
